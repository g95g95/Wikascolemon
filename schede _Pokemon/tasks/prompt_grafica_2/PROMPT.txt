Pixel art in the style of Game Boy Advance Pokémon Emerald battle sprites.
One row of 2 equal square cells on a solid flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, no shadows, no text, no borders.
The creature is the one in the attached artwork; keep its shapes and colours.
Cell 1: the creature seen from the front, turned slightly to the left, whole body visible, feet on the
bottom third of the cell.
Cell 2: the same creature seen from behind, turned slightly to the right (three-quarter back view),
larger than in cell 1 and cut by the bottom edge of the cell as in Emerald back sprites.
Dark outline, flat colours, at most 15 colours shared by both views, no gradients, no anti-aliasing,
crisp pixels.
